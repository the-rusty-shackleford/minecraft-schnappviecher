# Less conspicuous stalking and reliable escape

Rusty requested fixes for apparent target switching and poor swimming, then asked
for a sneakier creature because friends spotted it too easily. They authorized
release with the concurrent C.A.M.P. changes and a two-minute restart warning.

The victim remains fixed. The actual movement defect was replacing unfinished
random flee paths, compounded by water being penalized and underwater destinations
being accepted despite surface flotation. Keep usable escape routes, permit water,
choose surface endpoints and retain sufficient swimming momentum to cross a river.

Before theft, keep approximately twelve blocks behind the player's horizontal
heading; looking down must not collapse that separation. Use soft footsteps and
quiet giggles spaced thirty to sixty seconds apart. These are tuning defaults,
subject to playtest feedback. The stalking delay, watched freeze, reach and line
of sight requirements remain. Chase and ransom can still be noisy; all recovery,
visit frequency, cooldown and nonlethal behavior remain as previously decided.

This changes behavior and sound intensity, not the supplied model or recordings.
Server checks establish navigation, spacing, sound packets and watched fairness.
They do not establish subjective stealth or audio quality in a friend's setup.
