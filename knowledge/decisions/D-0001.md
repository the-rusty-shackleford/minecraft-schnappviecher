# Standalone creature and recoverable snack ransom

Rusty requested a new Schnappviecher mod and clarified that it must be separate from
the monster mod. It owns its own entity, model, AI, sounds and mechanics. There is
no Aberrant Mobs dependency and no shared-protocol change.

Rusty selected recoverable theft with a humorous payoff after escape. The first
implementation uses a one-snack processing fee (cookie, bread or apple). Combat
remains an alternative. Creative interactions require and consume no payment.

The encounter is nonlethal: three spaced player hits force surrender; environmental
damage never kills it. Each creature holds at most one stolen item. A world ledger
owns the actual ItemStack; the creature carries a reference and a display copy.
Replacement actors and stale chunk copies must consult that ledger before acting.
Normal saves and reloads preserve outstanding claims. This follows Minecraft's save
model; it does not claim an atomic transaction across files during a host crash.

Pure encounter rules are JDK-only and tested by partition. Inventory, persistence,
pathfinding, combat, titles and rendering are checked through the real game.

Release, pack updates and live-server restarts remain held for Rusty's explicit go.
