# A carried bag's contents are carried, so the creature may take from them

Part of Rusty's one change for every mod that looks at a player's inventory ("all things that see
your inventory also see your backpack"): the Carried protocol (`minecraft-carried`, its D-0001),
nested in this jar. Asked whether the creature may reach into a bag, Rusty: "yes".

D-0002 made every carried item eligible ("it should be able to steal anything"). The eligible
places are now every non-empty inventory slot, armour and offhand included as before, then every
non-empty cell of every bag the player carries (`Schnappviech.eligible`, one chance each, so a
full bag draws the creature's attention in proportion to what is in it). A worn bag is itself a
slot and can still be taken whole. `Claims.steal` takes one item from the chosen place through
`Carried.takeFrom`, which writes the bag once; the old slot form remains for the inventory. What is
handed back after a beating or a bribe is thrown to the player as before, and a pickup lands in a
bag when the inventory is full (Carried D-0002).

Verified by `TheftGameTests.aCarriedBagsItemsAreEligibleAndTakenOneAtATime` with Backpacks+ 0.6.0
on the gametest server: a bag in a pocket holding five diamonds makes two eligible places; a theft
from the diamonds' cell leaves four and the ledger holds one; an empty cell is refused. New code
with no 0.1.2 counterpart to fail on. The twenty earlier GameTests pass unchanged.
