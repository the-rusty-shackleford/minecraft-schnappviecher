# 0.1.2 verification — 2026-09-25

Rusty, on 0.1.1: the creature gets stuck at walls because it does not find the way to an
opening, and a target moving around flips it back and forth with no net movement.

## Reproduced first, on the unchanged creature

Two GameTests in `MovementGameTests`, run before any fix:

- `stalkRoundsAWallToReachItsPlace`: a thirty-block wall (x 33..62 at z 30, three high) between
  the creature at (48, 24) and its stand-off point (48, 36) behind a player at (48, 48) facing
  south, the way round past x 63. **Failed:** after 400 ticks the creature stood 1.3 blocks from
  the wall on a one-node route, `replans=0` (the same partial route every ten ticks, its target
  unchanged), 19.8 from the player. The first fixture had an 88-block wall; that one can never
  be rounded by anything, since vanilla caps a route's walked length at the follow range (48),

- `aSpinningPlayerDoesNotFlipTheCreatureBackAndForth`: the player turns a quarter every twenty
  ticks; the creature starts at its stand-off point. **Failed:** five reversals of heading in
  400 ticks, 24 blocks walked, ending 5 blocks from the player.

## The fix (D-0005) and what passed

`domain.Standoff` (six JUnit, partitions at the top): the band that keeps a post, the candidates
out of the view cone nearest the creature first, the ring's orientation from the yaw, bad
arguments, immutability. `Schnappviech.stalk` keeps its post while the victim stays within nine
to fifteen blocks of it, walks the first candidate a route reaches, else the route ending
nearest one and holds five seconds; the navigation's search budget is four times vanilla's.

- Both GameTests pass; the eighteen earlier ones unchanged: 20 in all. 16 JUnit.

## What the booth taught

The creature took the post east of the victim, not the one straight behind them, though that
one is nearer. Its log line (`stalk from … tried (0.5,-7.5):short 3.0/5 … (12.5,4.5):reaches/30`)
and a run with the route nodes printed showed vanilla's search giving up on the nearer posts
with a route ending at the wall's north face, while the eastern one was found straight through
the gap; a sixteen-fold budget changed nothing. Vanilla's A* overwrites a node's walked distance
on every visit and refuses nodes past the follow range, so a search that explores much of the
near side before the detour starves before the gap. The rule tolerates it (nearest reachable
post, whichever the search finds); the first booth check demanded the post straight behind and
was relaxed to what the rule promises. The first booth scene had the nearest post on the
creature's own side of the wall, which it took, rightly; the scene was redesigned so the whole
ring lies beyond the wall.
