# 0.1.1 verification — 2026-09-20

Executed the offline clean build with Java 21 and filesystem watching disabled:
ten JUnit tests and eighteen required real-server GameTests passed.
This is local verification, not a release/deployment record.

The initial escape test observed five abandoned unfinished routes in 120 ticks.
The river fixture failed to cross a sixteen-block-wide, four-deep channel.
Fixes retain a useful flee path, allow water destinations, choose the surface
instead of the riverbed and preserve swimming momentum. Four regressions cover
the fixed victim despite bystanders, river crossing, submerged-to-shore travel
and making eight blocks of separation while fleeing in open water.

The added stalking fixtures reproduced a waiting distance of 1.04 blocks while
the victim looked down, and a giggle at tick 82. Fixed tests verify a waiting
distance of at least nine blocks, first giggle no earlier than tick 600, one or
two giggles in 1201 ticks at volume no greater than 0.2, and watched theft
prevention with less than half a block of movement. Existing natural visit,
theft, escape, attacks, ransom and persistence checks also passed.

One sound fixture initially continued collecting packets after its player was
removed, masking the intended baseline failure. The collector now stops before
cleanup; the unchanged production AI then failed specifically on the early giggle.

No visual model or audio asset changed. No rendering booth was launched alongside
the existing host Minecraft client. Sound packets establish timing and volume,
not subjective audibility under headphones, shaders or another player's setup.
